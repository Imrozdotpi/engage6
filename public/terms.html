Who can register

Anyone who can sign in with a Google account and who meets the eligibility rules published with the event terms. You must give accurate details. Each person can be in one team only.

Teams and fees

Teams have 2 to 4 members. The registration fee is Rs 250 per team member, paid once by the team leader through Razorpay. Paying locks the team roster. Registration closes on 27 October 2026, 6:00 PM IST.

The event

The hackathon runs for four hours on the NCU campus in Gurugram. The problem statement is given on the spot. Teams may use any AI tools, subject to the event rules and each tool's own terms. Repositories must be submitted before the four hours end.
